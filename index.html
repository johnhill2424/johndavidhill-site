Bet Slip Notifier

A personal automation, built and operated by John Hill, that looks up sportsbook odds for a bet and sends the result as a text message.

This is not a public product, app, or service with user sign-up. It has exactly one recipient: the person who owns and deploys it. When a bet description (e.g. "Chargers +3.5") is submitted, the system looks up the best currently available odds across major sportsbooks and sends the result by SMS to the phone number its owner configured at deploy time.

There is no subscriber list, no marketing use, and no distribution of messages to any number other than the owner's own.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the phone number the account owner configures as the notification destination, and the bet text submitted for a lookup.

  • The phone number is used solely to deliver bet-odds results to that owner.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with any third party or affiliate for marketing purposes.
  • Bet text is sent to a third-party sports-odds API solely to look up current prices; it is not used for any other purpose.
  • Data may be retained for the operator's own recordkeeping (e.g. bet history) and is not shared beyond that.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact address below.

SMS Terms

  • Program: Bet-slip odds notifications.
  • Message frequency: Varies — approximately one message per bet submitted by the account owner.
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Opt-in: Consent occurs when the account owner configures their own phone number as the notification destination at deployment time (--context notification_phone=...). There is no separate sign-up flow because the sender and the sole recipient are the same person.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP to stop receiving messages at any time. Reply HELP for help.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
